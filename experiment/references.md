1. **Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest, Clifford Stein, Introduction to Algorithms (3rd Edition).**
   - Comprehensive coverage of searching and sorting algorithms, their analysis, and applications.

2. **Robert Sedgewick & Kevin Wayne, Algorithms (4th Edition).**
   - Practical explanations and visualizations of searching and sorting techniques.

3. **Wikipedia: Sorting Algorithm**
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorting_algorithm
   - Overview, types, and comparisons of sorting algorithms.

4. **Wikipedia: Search Algorithm**
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Search_algorithm
   - Theory and examples of searching algorithms.

5. **NPTEL: Data Structures and Algorithms**
   - https://nptel.ac.in/courses/106/106/106106127/
   - Video lectures and notes on searching and sorting.
